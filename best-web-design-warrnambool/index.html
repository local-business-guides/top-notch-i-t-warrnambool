What to prepare and what the process covers

A little preparation can make it easier to decide what belongs on your site. Gather the details customers commonly need, such as your service descriptions, contact information and any existing website content you want to keep. You don’t need to arrive with every page written. A conversation about your goals can help identify what’s missing.

A simple checklist to bring to an initial discussion:

  • The main services you want people to find
  • The action you want visitors to take
  • Any concerns with your current website
  • Whether you need hosting, maintenance or help with updates

Avoid choosing a design based only on appearance, or assuming a site will be easy to manage without asking. If your current website is hard to update, slow to explain what you do or awkward on mobile, those are useful details to mention.
