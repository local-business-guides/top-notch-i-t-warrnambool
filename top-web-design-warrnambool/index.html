A simple checklist before you begin

Top Web Design Warrnambool can cover planning, page design, content organisation and the practical setup of a website. The exact work depends on whether you need a new site or changes to an existing one. Bring the information that will help the designer understand your services and the customers you want to reach.

Before your first conversation, prepare:
- A short description of your business and its services.
- The pages or details you want visitors to find easily.
- Any existing website text, images or brand materials you can provide.
- A list of issues with your current site, if you have one.
- Your preference for handling future edits and maintenance.

Avoid starting with a long list of decorative features. First make sure the core information is accurate and easy to follow. It’s also helpful to decide who will approve content and supply updates, so the project doesn’t stall while those details are being gathered.
