What a new business website should do

Website Development Warrnambool is a practical choice when you need a new site or want to replace a basic online presence with something easier to use. A good website should help a visitor understand what you offer, find the information that matters and know how to contact you. It should also be comfortable to read on a phone, where many people browse between other tasks.

Before starting, think about the questions customers ask most often. Which services do you want to explain? What should someone do after reading about them? A clear answer to those questions can help shape the pages and content, instead of leaving visitors to guess where to go.

What the development work can include

The scope depends on what your business needs. Website development can include planning the page structure, designing the site, preparing it for mobile screens and setting up a WordPress website. Hosting, maintenance, SEO and ongoing support are also available from Top Notch I.T.

A short preparation checklist can make the first discussion more productive:

  • Write down your main services and the people you want to reach.
  • Gather your business contact details and any existing site materials.
  • Note what visitors should be able to do, such as make an enquiry.
  • List any concerns with your current online presence.

Try not to cram every detail onto one page or use vague service descriptions. If your site is hard to update, doesn't display well on a phone or leaves visitors unsure how to reach you, it may be time to get professional help.
