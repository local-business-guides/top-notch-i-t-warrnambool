Signs your website may need a redesign

Website Redesign Warrnambool can make sense when the site you already have no longer gives customers a clear picture of your business. Perhaps important services take too much effort to find, the pages are uncomfortable to read on a phone, or you struggle to make a simple change. A redesign is a chance to reconsider the layout and content with the people using the site in mind.

Before deciding, take a fresh look at the pages you rely on. Can a new visitor tell what you do? Are your contact details easy to spot? Does the site still describe your services accurately? These checks can help separate the issues that need attention from changes that would only be cosmetic.

What a redesign can cover

A redesign can involve reworking the page structure, refreshing the appearance and making information easier to follow on different screens. Depending on your needs, Top Notch I.T can also help with WordPress websites, hosting, maintenance, SEO and ongoing support. The right scope depends on what is already on your site and what you want it to do next.

Before getting started, use this quick review:

  • Identify pages that are out of date or difficult to understand.
  • Check how the site looks and reads on a mobile phone.
  • Gather the service details and contact information that should remain.
  • Make a note of anything you want visitors to do, such as enquire.

One common mistake is changing the look without fixing unclear wording or hard-to-find information. Another is removing useful details without deciding where customers will find them. If you aren't sure what to keep, a review with a web professional can help you make a more considered plan.
